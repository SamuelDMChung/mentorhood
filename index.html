ABOUT MENTORHOOD

Growing confident math learners since 2009.

Mentorhood began in Hong Kong, founded by educators Karen Lai and Gigi Lam, with a vision to strengthen students’ understanding rather than rely on memorization alone.

A visual-first way of teaching

The program developed around model drawing, math games, interactive activities and teachers trained to help children reason through unfamiliar problems.

Over time, Mentorhood expanded its teaching network and introduced online classes alongside in-person learning.
