Singapore Math Method

from Mentorhood

What Is Visible Thinking Math?

Students are provided with different visual ways of looking at math with our bar modelling approach.

Their learning journey begins with the concrete and pictorial stages, followed by the abstract stage to enable them to learn mathematics meaningfully.

This approach encourages advanced (higher-order) thinking, communication of mathematical ideas and problem solving.

Our approach Concrete › Pictorial › Abstract

What Is Singapore “Thinking Bar Models”?

The Thinking Bar Model approach is a systematic method for representing word problems and number relationships in a visual way. Our students use rectangular bars to represent known and unknown quantities, simplifying the problem-solving process.

Ages and Stages: Our Process

Creative Math

Age 4–6 | Kindergarten 1 and 2

Explore mathematical ideas through music, art, activities and games, establishing a foundation for a love of learning.

Visible Thinking Math

Age 6–12 | Grades 1–6

Use bar models to organize and analyze questions, develop confidence and solve increasingly complex problems.

Our Inception

Our Inception

Mentorhood Math was founded in 2009 in Hong Kong by Ms. Karen and Ms. Gigi to help children build a stronger math foundation. In 2011, the first version of Mentorhood Mathematics launched with the Model Drawing Method.

In 2012, the first teaching manual was written. In 2014, the first franchise learning center opened, and in 2016 new Creative Mathematics and Visible Thinking Mathematics curricula were developed.

Mentorhood expanded online learning in 2020 and has continued to grow its educators and teaching resources.
